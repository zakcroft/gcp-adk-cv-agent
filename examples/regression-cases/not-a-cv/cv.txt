Project Phoenix — Q3 Status Report

Summary
The migration of the customer billing service to the new platform is on
track for the October release. Two of the four workstreams are complete;
the remaining two are in progress with no critical blockers.

Progress this quarter
• Billing API migration completed and deployed to staging
• Data reconciliation scripts written and tested against production copies
• Load testing began in week 9; initial results within acceptable limits
• Customer communication plan drafted and under review with marketing

Risks
The reconciliation of legacy invoice records is taking longer than
estimated because of undocumented edge cases in the old schema. A decision
is needed by the end of the month on whether to extend the timeline or
descope the historical data older than five years.

Next steps
Complete load testing, finish the reconciliation workstream, and present
the go/no-go recommendation at the November steering meeting.
