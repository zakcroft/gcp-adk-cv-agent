Mid-level Software Engineer

We're looking for a mid-level software engineer to join our platform team.

front-end developers, and help keep our PostgreSQL databases healthy.

Key responsibilities:

• Support production systems, including occasional on-call

Requirements:
• 3+ years of professional software development experience
• Solid Python, ideally with Django
• Working knowledge of JavaScript and React
• Experience with relational databases, ideally PostgreSQL
• Familiarity with Git and standard code-review workflows
