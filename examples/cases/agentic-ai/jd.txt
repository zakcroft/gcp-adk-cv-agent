Agentic AI Engineer

We're building a modern, cloud-native platform and evolving towards an
agentic-first engineering model, where AI agents perform meaningful
delivery and operational work alongside a small, highly experienced
engineering team.

We're looking for an Agentic AI Engineer to design, build and own the
production platform that enables AI agents to operate safely and
effectively. This is not simply about integrating LLMs, it is about
creating the infrastructure, tooling and governance that allows autonomous
systems to deliver real business value in production.

A key part of this role is knowing when an AI agent is the right solution
and when a deterministic workflow is the better engineering choice. You'll
help define this balance and play a central role in shaping how AI is
adopted across the organisation.

What you'll do
• Design, build and own the production platform that enables AI agents to
  perform real engineering and operational tasks.
• Develop the tooling, orchestration, guardrails, evaluation frameworks
  and execution patterns required for reliable autonomous systems.
• Make architectural decisions around where agentic AI adds genuine value
  and where traditional deterministic workflows are more appropriate.
• Build evaluation frameworks for measuring the quality, reliability and
  safety of non-deterministic AI outputs.
• Work closely with engineering teams to ensure autonomous systems can
  safely interact with code, infrastructure and business-critical
  workflows.
• Deliver production-ready AI agents that automate complex engineering and
  operational processes.
• Help shape and evolve an AI-first operating model across the wider
  technology function.

Technology
You'll work with modern LLMs, agent orchestration frameworks, evaluation
tooling, cloud-native infrastructure (AWS or similar), APIs and
contemporary AI development ecosystems.

What we're looking for
• Proven experience building and operating production-grade agentic AI
  systems — not just prototypes or prompt engineering demonstrations.
• Strong understanding of agent orchestration, tool design, guardrails and
  evaluation for non-deterministic systems.
• Experience designing secure autonomous workflows that interact with
  applications, codebases or cloud infrastructure.
• Excellent judgement around when agentic AI is the right solution versus
  when deterministic automation delivers a better outcome.
• Strong software engineering and cloud architecture fundamentals.

Nice to have
• Experience working with leading foundation model providers and modern AI
  development ecosystems.
• Background in applied AI research or LLM experimentation.
• Experience with retrieval systems, RAG, context engineering or knowledge
  systems.
• Familiarity with cloud-native AI deployment and MLOps practices.
