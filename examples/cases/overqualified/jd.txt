Support Engineer (Junior)
NorthBridge SaaS — Edinburgh, UK (office-based)
£26,000 - £30,000

About NorthBridge
NorthBridge provides scheduling and rota software for healthcare
providers — 300+ clinics and care organisations across the UK rely on us
to get the right staff in the right place. We're a stable, profitable
company of 45 people, and our support team is the front line of our
reputation: customers regularly name them in reviews.

The role
Our support engineers investigate customer-reported issues, reproduce
bugs, and work directly with the development team to get them fixed. This
is a technical role, not a call-centre role: you'll spend most of your
day in logs, databases, and bug reports rather than on the phone.

What you'll be doing
• Investigating and triaging customer-reported technical issues
• Reproducing bugs in our test environments and writing clear, complete
  reports for the development team
• Running SQL queries to investigate data issues and correct records
  under change control
• Reading application logs to trace what actually happened
• Writing and updating support documentation and runbooks
• Escalating incidents appropriately and communicating status to
  customers in plain English

What we're looking for
• Some experience with SQL and reading application logs
• A methodical, evidence-first approach to troubleshooting
• Clear written communication — most of your output is written
• Basic scripting ability (Python or similar) is a plus
• Patience and a customer-first attitude; our users are clinicians and
  care coordinators, not technologists
• Comfortable working fully office-based in central Edinburgh

Growth
Support engineers who want to move toward development get real routes to
do so — two of our current developers started on the support desk.

Benefits
23 days holiday + bank holidays, healthcare cash plan, subsidised gym
membership, season-ticket loan, Friday team lunches.
