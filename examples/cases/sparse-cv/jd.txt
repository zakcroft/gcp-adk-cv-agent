Backend Engineer (Mid-level)
Halcyon Logistics — Bristol, UK (office-first, 1 day remote)
£45,000 - £58,000 depending on experience

About Halcyon
Halcyon builds routing and tracking software for freight companies. Our
platform plans and monitors thousands of vehicle movements a day for
hauliers across the UK and Ireland. We're a 60-person company with a
12-person engineering team, and our systems genuinely matter to our
customers' operations — when we're down, trucks stand still.

The role
We need a backend engineer to develop and maintain our core Java
services: route planning, tracking ingestion, and customer-facing APIs.
You'll own features end to end — design, build, test, ship, and support
in production.

Responsibilities
• Design and build features across our Java/Spring microservices
• Write and maintain automated tests as part of everyday development
• Model and query data in PostgreSQL; keep our schemas sane
• Take part in code reviews and our weekly design sessions
• Join the on-call rotation (roughly one week in six, paid)
• Collaborate with product managers and the frontend team on delivery
• Help us keep our CI pipelines fast and reliable

Requirements
• 3+ years of professional backend development experience
• Strong Java; Spring Boot experience strongly preferred
• Solid SQL and real experience with relational databases in production
• Experience with testing frameworks (JUnit or similar) and a testing
  mindset
• Comfortable with Git, pull-request workflows, and CI pipelines
• Able to communicate technical decisions clearly in writing

Nice to have
• Exposure to messaging systems (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar)
• Docker and container-based deployment experience
• Experience in logistics, mapping, or other operationally-critical
  domains

What we offer
25 days holiday, annual bonus scheme, pension matching to 6%, paid
on-call, £800 personal development budget, free parking at the Bristol
office.

We review every application personally — no keyword filters — and aim to
respond within a week.
