Junior Data Analyst
DataFirst Insights — Manchester, UK (hybrid, 2 days in office)
£28,000 - £34,000 + benefits

About us
DataFirst Insights helps retail brands understand their customers through
data. From our Manchester office we support over 40 clients across the UK
and Europe, turning raw sales, marketing, and loyalty data into clear,
actionable reporting. We're a team of 25 and growing, and we invest
properly in developing junior analysts — structured mentoring, a training
budget, and a clear progression path to senior analyst within 2-3 years.

The role
We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our client services team.
Working day to day with two senior analysts, you'll prepare, analyse, and
present data for a portfolio of retail clients. This is a genuinely
hands-on analytical role: you will be in the data every day.

What you'll be doing
• Cleaning and preparing client datasets in SQL and Python
• Building and maintaining dashboards and reports in Power BI
• Running exploratory analysis to answer client questions
• Presenting findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders, in writing
  and on client calls
• Supporting senior analysts on larger modelling and segmentation projects
• Contributing to our internal documentation and analysis playbooks

What we're looking for
• Working knowledge of SQL and at least one of Python or R
• Experience with a BI tool (Power BI, Tableau, or Looker)
• Comfort with statistics fundamentals (distributions, significance
  testing, confidence intervals)
• Strong Excel skills — pivot tables, lookups, charts
• Clear written and verbal communication; you can explain a finding to
  someone who has never seen a database
• A methodical, detail-first approach to checking your own work
• Degree in a numerate or analytical discipline preferred, but we care
  more about demonstrable skills than certificates

Nice to have
• Experience analysing marketing or sales data
• Familiarity with Google Analytics or other web analytics tools
• Exposure to dbt, BigQuery, or cloud data warehouses
• Experience presenting to clients or senior stakeholders

Benefits
25 days holiday + bank holidays, private healthcare, £1,000 annual
training budget, hybrid working, cycle-to-work scheme, regular team
socials.

DataFirst Insights is an equal opportunities employer. We welcome
applications from all backgrounds and are happy to discuss flexible
working arrangements.
