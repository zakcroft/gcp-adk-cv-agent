Junior Data Analyst
DataFirst Insights — Manchester, UK (hybrid)

About us
DataFirst Insights helps retail clients understand their customers through
data. Our analysts turn raw sales and marketing data into clear, actionable
reports.

The role
We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our client services team.
You will work with senior analysts to prepare, analyse, and present data
for retail clients.

Responsibilities
• Clean and prepare datasets in SQL and Python
• Build dashboards and reports in Power BI
• Run exploratory analysis to answer client questions
• Present findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders
• Support senior analysts on larger modelling projects

Requirements
• Working knowledge of SQL and at least one of Python or R
• Experience with a BI tool (Power BI, Tableau, or Looker)
• Comfort with statistics fundamentals (distributions, significance testing)
• Strong Excel skills
• Clear written and verbal communication
• Degree in a numerate or analytical discipline preferred

Nice to have
• Experience analysing marketing or sales data
• Familiarity with Google Analytics
